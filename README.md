# OmniVideo - Multilingual Video Generator & Studio

OmniVideo is an AI-powered digital video creation studio specializing in Telugu and English content across YouTube (16:9 / Shorts), Instagram Reels (9:16), and TikTok with full offline access.

## Features

- **Interactive Live Video Canvas**: Real-time playhead timeline scrubbing, playback speed adjustments (0.5x – 2.0x), and looping within trimmed boundaries.
- **Precision Trimming**: Start/end range adjusters, snap-to-playhead markers, and duration presets for Reels (15s), TikTok (30s), and longform.
- **On-Screen Text & Banners**: Customizable placement (Top, Center, Bottom), styles (Neon Glow, Solid, Frosted Glass, Outline), and curated Telugu hook presets.
- **Telugu Meme Studio**: Preloaded iconic comedy templates (Brahmanandam Shock, Ali Reaction, Venky Victory, AI Mindblown) with customizable top/bottom punchlines and timing controls.
- **Google Photos & Search Media Sourcing**: Aerial 4K drone footage, Vizag coastal waves, and studio backgrounds to apply to canvas or import into your asset library.
- **Audio Levels & Voice Clarity**: Dual-track mixing for original audio and voiceover with AI speech enhancement and noise isolation.
- **GPU-Accelerated Color Grading**: Vivid Punch, Cyberpunk Neon, Cinematic Teal & Orange, Monochrome Noir, and Golden Hour Sunset with fine-tuned intensity controls.
- **Multi-Platform Reframing**: Seamlessly switch aspect ratios between 9:16 Vertical Reel, 16:9 Horizontal Widescreen, 1:1 Square Feed, and 4:5 Portrait.
- **Multilingual AI Suite (Google Gemini)**:
  - Concise bullet summaries in English and authentic Telugu script (తెలుగులో ముఖ్యాంశాలు).
  - YouTube SEO title, description, and chapter timestamps.
  - Instagram Reels caption, hook, and 12+ trending hashtags.
  - TikTok 3-second viral hook and search tags.
  - B-roll visual pacing guide and on-screen title suggestions.
- **Offline Video Vault**: Exported video packages saved locally to browser storage with simulated H.264/AAC MP4 specs, resolution, and downloadable metadata packages.

## Running Locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

The application runs on `http://0.0.0.0:3000`.
